## 📚 Supplemental Materials Continued

We proceed with providing overviews for the supplemental material documents included in the `user_study_resources` subdirectory. As the name suggests, all materials pertain to the conducted user study crafted to assess the proposed autonomous content adjustment (prototype) system. Namely,

- `participant_consent_form.pdf`: The consent form reviewed and signed by all participants before beginning the user study, as outlined in the main paper. The conducted study was approved by the Duke University IRB.
- `participant_pre_survey.pdf`: Before beginning the study, participants completed a short pre-survey to collect demographic information and learn about their prior exposure to augmented and extended reality applications.
- `user_study_pre_instructions.pdf`: Additionally, before putting on the AR head-mounted display system, participants were briefly introduced to the study. These instructions, presented in slideshow format, were reviewed; participants had the opportunity to ask clarifying questions.
- `participant_post_survey.pdf`: Upon completing the user study session, participants were asked to fill out a short post-survey regarding overall perception of the system variants and their experience.
